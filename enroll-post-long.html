ENROLLMENT SCHEDULE
November 3 – 30, 2026

For continuing College students. Sign in using either way:

OPTION A

Tatak Goldenian App

  1. Update to the latest version (2.0.0) in the Google Play Store.
  2. Open the app and unlock it with your MPIN or fingerprint.
  3. Tap your student card. The Student Portal opens, already signed in.
OPTION B

Web Browser

  1. Go to
    portal.golden-success.com
  2. Type your student number and tap Sign in.
  3. Enter the 6-digit code sent to your school email.

HOW TO APPLY

  1. 1
    Tap the Enroll tab, then tap Apply for 2nd Semester 2026–2027.
  2. 2
    Check your mobile number and email.
  3. 3
    Fill in your emergency contact (name, number, address).
  4. 4
    Tick your subjects, including any back subjects listed.
  5. 5
    Tap Add up to check your units (max 30, or 36 if graduating).
  6. 6
    Read each policy and turn on its switch.
  7. 7
    Tap Submit application.
After you submit, submit your clearance to the registrar. It is one of the requirements for approval.
You will get an email from mis@golden-success.com when your application is submitted, and again once the registrar approves, returns or rejects it. Check its status anytime in the Enroll tab.
